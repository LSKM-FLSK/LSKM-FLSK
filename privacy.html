Privacy Policy

LSK M – Social Media Analytics

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LSK M – Social Media Analytics, operated by LSK Employee, collects, uses and manages information when the application is used.

LSK M – Social Media Analytics is an internal application designed for in-house social media analytics, data collection and performance reporting.

2. Information We Process

When authorised social media accounts are connected to the application, we may process information made available through the relevant platform's authorised API or developer services.

This may include:

  • Social media account information
  • Post and video information
  • Views
  • Likes
  • Comments
  • Shares
  • Engagement statistics
  • Watch-time and performance information
  • Other analytics made available by the relevant platform

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through the application is used for internal analytics and reporting purposes, including measuring social media performance, comparing content performance and improving internal social media decision-making.

4. Third-Party Platforms

The application may connect to third-party social media platforms using their authorised APIs and developer services.

Information received from these platforms is subject to the permissions granted to the application and the policies and requirements of the relevant platform.

5. Data Sharing

Information collected through LSK M – Social Media Analytics is intended for internal use. We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary for the operation, storage or functionality of the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is collected, including internal analytics and reporting, or where retention is required by applicable obligations.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the application from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

8. Data Deletion and Requests

Requests relating to information processed by the application, including requests concerning access or deletion where applicable, can be submitted using the contact information below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application, its functionality, third-party platform requirements or applicable obligations.
