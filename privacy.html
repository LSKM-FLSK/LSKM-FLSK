Privacy Policy Last updated: 5 September 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and processed through LSK M – Social Media Analytics ("the App"). The App is an internal social media analytics and data collection tool operated by an authorised LSK employee for business and analytical purposes. 1. Information We Collect Through authorised integrations with supported social media platforms, the App may collect information made available through the relevant platform's APIs. Depending on the platform, permissions and API access available, this may include: social media account identifiers; usernames and profile information; follower counts; posts and videos; post titles or captions; publication dates; post URLs; views; likes; comments; shares; saves; watch-time measurements; follower changes; engagement measurements; and other analytics information made available by the relevant platform. The information actually collected depends on the platform and permissions approved for the App. 2. How We Use Information Information collected through the App is used for internal business and analytical purposes, including: monitoring social media performance; analysing individual post performance; maintaining historical performance records; comparing performance over time; analysing engagement; analysing follower growth; identifying trends; generating performance insights; and improving social media content and strategy. 3. Internal Access The App is intended for authorised internal use. Information collected through the App is not intended to be made publicly available. Access to the analytics system is restricted to authorised personnel who require access for legitimate business purposes. 4. Social Media Authorisation The App uses the relevant social media platform's official authentication and authorisation mechanisms where required. The App does not request or store social media passwords. A user may revoke the App's access through the relevant social media platform. After access is revoked, the App may no longer be able to collect new information from that account. 5. Historical Analytics The App may retain historical analytics information. For example, performance measurements may be recorded at different points in time so that changes in views, engagement and other metrics can be analysed. This historical information may be retained even when the current performance figures of a social media post subsequently change. 6. Data Storage Information collected through the App may be stored using systems and services required to operate the analytics application, including databases, automation services and hosting infrastructure. Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. 7. Data Sharing Information collected through the App is not sold. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the App, such as database, hosting, automation and infrastructure providers. Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide the services used to operate the App. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the security or integrity of the App. 8. Third-Party Platforms The App may interact with TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and other third-party platforms. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and terms governing their services. The App's collection of information through those platforms is limited to information available through authorised APIs and approved permissions. 9. Data Retention Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's internal analytics functionality, maintain historical performance records and fulfil legitimate business and legal requirements. Where information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymised where appropriate. 10. Data Rights Depending on the applicable law and the nature of the information concerned, individuals may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights to: request access to personal information; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion; request restriction of processing; object to certain processing; and exercise other applicable data-protection rights. Requests can be made using the contact details below. 11. Security Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the App. However, no electronic storage system or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 12. Children's Privacy The App is intended for authorised internal business use and is not designed to knowingly collect information from children for independent use of the App. 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App, its functionality or its data-processing practices change. The "Last updated" date will be amended when significant changes are made. 14.
