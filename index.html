LSK M – Social Media Analytics

Internal social media analytics and performance reporting.

About

LSK M – Social Media Analytics is an internal social media analytics application operated by LSK Employee.

The application is designed for in-house data collection and analysis of social media performance across supported platforms.

The application may collect and organise analytics including:

  • Post views
  • Likes
  • Comments
  • Shares
  • Engagement
  • Watch-time data
  • Post performance
  • Account performance

This information is used to analyse social media content performance and improve internal reporting and decision-making.
