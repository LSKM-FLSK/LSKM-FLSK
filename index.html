LSK M – Social Media Analytics

LSK M – Social Media Analytics is an internal social media analytics and data collection application used for authorised LSK business and analytical purposes.

About the Application

The application collects and analyses social media performance information from supported platforms through authorised APIs. This information is used to monitor performance, analyse trends, maintain historical analytics and support social media strategy.
